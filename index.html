03 / HOW TO READ THIS

A little context matters.

Dates, not countdowns

The horizontal axis is the date of the forecast session, which sometimes precedes publication. The vertical axis is the predicted calendar year. “Years moved” measures change from each category’s own first recorded estimate.

Ranges are scenarios

The optional shaded band is the focused timeline’s published plausible range. These are qualitative planning ranges, not statistical confidence intervals. Steps show revisions at dated updates; gaps mean no estimate was recorded.

Keep the definitions honest

Original RSI is separate from the later three-part RSI framework. The original FDVR history continues as Neural FDVR; Hybrid FDVR starts on 29 September. New categories begin when first explicitly tracked.

Source notes & historical inconsistencies

Values come from each post’s current forecast table, including the early image tables and the later embedded Datawrapper datasets. A later “first week” or “previous week” column does not overwrite an earlier post’s own values. The 20 June undifferentiated RSI estimate is 2031, while later comparison tables refer to 2032 for full RSI. The 14 July retrospective comparison also differs from the 7 July current estimates. Both are preserved as originally stated.

The original RSI observation is available in the legend, but is hidden by default because it is not directly comparable to autonomous RSI. UBI is first explicitly tracked on 14 July; abundance categories on 18 August; fusion on 1 September; Hybrid FDVR on 29 September; superconductors on 27 October. Qualitative “Now” and “Achieved” labels for weak RSI are kept out of the numeric chart.

Forecasts depend on one another and on the models and prompts used by the publication. A revision can reflect changed interpretation or model choice as well as new evidence. This independent visualisation does not endorse the forecasts or fact-check the underlying news. Archived here as retrieved on ; updates are manual.
